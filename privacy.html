Privacy Policy for Mergefall

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Jaudat Al-Husein ("we", "us") handles information in the game Mergefall (the "Game") on Android and on the web.

Information we collect

The Game does not ask for your name, email, location or any other personal information, and it does not need an account. Your progress (stars, coins, settings, statistics) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

Android app: the Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads. Users in the EEA and UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Web version: when you play on a game website such as CrazyGames or Poki, ads are served by that website under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The Game currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added, they will be processed by Google Play, and we will never receive your payment details.

Children

The Game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can erase all progress stored on your device from the Game's Settings screen (Reset all progress), or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
